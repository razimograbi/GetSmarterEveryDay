Privacy policy

Get Smarter Every Day (“Smarts”) · Last updated 31 October 2026

Smarts is a free picture quiz. This page explains what it stores about you, why, and how to see or delete it. In short: only what the game needs to save your progress. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or trackers.

What we store

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID. We never see your Google password.
  • Your game progress: the rounds you play, each answer you give, whether it was right and how long it took. This powers your scores, streaks and statistics.
  • Your settings: for example whether “Auto next” is on.
  • Question reports you send, including any note you write.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It holds a random code, not personal information.

How it's used

Only to run the game: to sign you in, save and show your progress, remember your settings and fix questions you report. Your data is not shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to profile you.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Google, to sign you in (Google Identity Services).
  • Google Fonts, for the app's fonts.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons, flagcdn.com and Esri, for some question pictures and maps when they aren't stored on our server.

These services receive the usual technical details of a web request (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies. We don't send them your account or progress.

How long we keep it

Your account and progress are kept until you delete your account. Expired sign-in sessions are removed automatically. Server backups are kept for up to 14 days.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Progress page → “Download my data”.
  • Sign out everywhere: Progress page → “Sign out on all devices”.
  • Delete your account: Progress page → “Delete my account”. Your account, rounds, answers, settings and sessions are deleted immediately. Question reports you sent are kept without your name, so the question can still be fixed. Copies in backups disappear within 14 days.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. Sign-in sessions are stored as hashed codes, and the server limits how fast requests can be made.

Children

Smarts is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
