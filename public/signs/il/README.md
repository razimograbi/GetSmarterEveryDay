# Israeli road signs

Official road sign drawings of the Israel Ministry of Transport, named by their sign
number in the official sign catalogue (for example `302.svg` is sign 302, Stop).

Source: Wikimedia Commons, files named "Israel road sign <number>.svg"
(<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Diagrams_of_road_signs_of_Israel>).
Most are public domain in Israel as works of the Israeli government (`PD-IsraelGov`), but
some are redrawn versions under CC BY-SA, which need their author credited. The Photo credits
page (`/credits`, from `legal/credits.json`) lists each file's author and licence.
