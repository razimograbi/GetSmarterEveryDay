Privacy policy

Smarts · Last updated 8 November 2026

Smarts is a free picture quiz. This page explains what it stores about you, why, and how to see or delete it. In short: only what the game needs to save your progress and to count how many people play. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or third-party trackers.

What we store

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID. We never see your Google password.
  • Your game progress: the rounds you play, each answer you give, whether it was right and how long it took. This powers your scores, streaks and statistics.
  • What you do in the game: when you start and finish a round, with details such as the topic and score. If Smarts later adds helpers, ads you choose to watch or purchases, using or buying them is recorded the same way.
  • Your public profile: the name, avatar and country you choose, and your level, which comes from the rounds you finish. Other players see them on the Daily Challenge boards, in duels and in friends lists. You can hide yourself from public boards; your friends and duel partners still see you.
  • Playing with others: who your friends are, your Daily Challenge results and rank, and your duels with their scores and answers.
  • Reminders, if you turn them on: the address your browser gives us for sending notifications. It points to your browser's push service (such as Google's, Apple's or Mozilla's). Reminders are encrypted so that only your browser can read them.
  • Your settings: for example your language, whether “Auto next” is on and which reminders you want. We also keep your time zone, so your streak and reminders follow your day.
  • Question reports you send, including any note you write.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It holds a random code, not personal information.

Your device also keeps a few things for you: the language you picked, pictures it has already downloaded, and the app's own files, so it opens quickly and can be installed on your home screen. These stay on your device.

How it's used

To run the game: to sign you in, save and show your progress, remember your settings and fix questions you report. We also count, in total, how many people play each day and month, how many come back, and which features are used, so we know what to improve. Your data is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used to profile you.

We use your data because you asked to play (to provide the game) and because counting how the game is used is needed to keep it running and improve it.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Google, to sign you in (Google Identity Services).
  • Google Fonts, for the app's fonts.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons, flagcdn.com and Esri, for some question pictures, country flags and maps when they aren't stored on our server.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla), only if you turn on reminders, to deliver them.

These services receive the usual technical details of a web request (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies. We don't send them your account or progress.

How long we keep it

Your account, profile, progress and game events are kept until you delete your account. If you played in someone else's duel, their duel keeps your score after you delete your account, shown as a deleted player. Expired sign-in sessions are removed automatically. Server backups are kept for up to 14 days.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Progress page → “Download my data”.
  • Sign out everywhere: Progress page → “Sign out on all devices”.
  • Hide from public boards: Progress page → your profile → “Show me on public boards”.
  • Remove a friend on the Friends page, and turn reminders off under Reminders on the Progress page or in your browser's settings.
  • Delete your account: Progress page → “Delete my account”. Your account, profile, friends, rounds, answers, game events, Daily results, duels you started, reminder sign-ups, settings and sessions are deleted immediately. Question reports you sent are kept without your name, so the question can still be fixed. Copies in backups disappear within 14 days.
  • Anything else, such as correcting your data: write to us (below).

Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. Sign-in sessions are stored as hashed codes, and the server limits how fast requests can be made.

Children

Smarts is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.
